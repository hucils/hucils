<h2>I. Preface</h2>

<p>
  I am an autistic trans woman with a particular fondness for artificial intelligence<br />
  and for the slightly improvisational craft of <i>AI-assisted coding</i>:<br />
  letting curiosity set the course and rigour follow close behind.
</p>

<p>
  My work wanders between disciplines: embedded robotics, graphics engines,<br />
  functional system configuration and the preservation of the online worlds I grew up in.<br />
  Each project here began as a question I could not leave alone.
</p>
